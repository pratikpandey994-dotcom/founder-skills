# Case 03 — good technology, wrong shape

The rice-straw film case. Recorded verdict 2026-09-19 was PIVOT at 3.3, with the
alternative being licence the film to an existing manufacturer rather than build a plant.

Tests that the skill reaches for licensing instead of company-building when the team
cannot manufacture — the behaviour generic startup tools do not have.

**Idea:** Two materials scientists in Pune. Biodegradable packaging film from rice straw
that matches regular plastic film in lab tests. Want to turn it into a business.

**Intake answers:** Food packaging, undecided. Biggest batch 200g, solution-cast in petri
dishes. Tensile similar to LDPE, composts in ~90 days. No moisture-barrier or heat-seal
testing. Lab only, not to any standard. Maybe Rs 400/kg in lab chemicals, uncertain. Rice
straw free from farms near Pune. Published a paper last year, no patent, DST grant through
the university, nothing signed. India, Maharashtra first. No buyer conversations. Know of
PLA films and paper bags, no specific companies. Both research-only; co-founder's brother
runs a small printing business. Part-time, ~10 hours a week each. Rs 5 lakh total, cannot
leave jobs, open to grants. Would like a real company but licensing is fine. Know nothing
about manufacturing.
